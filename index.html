Practice patterns & tips

Start slowly. Move your hand down on the beat and up on “and,” even when you skip a strum.

Choosing a pattern replaces the current steps. Customize every step in Settings → Edit Pattern.

Standard counts (1–8, e, and, a, up, down, rest, change, ready) use precisely scheduled audio. At fast tempos they speed up to fit. Other custom phrases use browser speech, which may be late or skip when busy. Wired audio avoids Bluetooth delay.

Space: start/stop · T: tap tempo · Arrow keys: change tempo. Practice pauses when you switch tabs to prevent unreliable background timing.
